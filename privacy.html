Privacy Policy

LidShift for Mac · Last updated: 13 September 2026

The short version

LidShift collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs — and it cannot connect to the internet at all: the app runs in Apple’s App Sandbox with network access switched off.

To animate your desktop, LidShift captures a single image of your built-in display. That image exists only in memory, only for as long as the effect runs, and is never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. I never receive it.

2. The lid angle sensor

While LidShift is active, it reads the opening angle of your MacBook’s hinge from the built-in sensor about thirty times a second. Only the current angle in whole degrees is used, to drive the effect and to show it in the menu bar panel. Angle readings are not stored, not logged and not transmitted.

3. Screen capture

Every effect except Dim needs macOS’s Screen Recording permission. LidShift only asks for it when you choose to allow it — in the first-run guide or with the button in the menu — and you can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.

With the permission granted, LidShift works like this:

  • One still image per close. When the lid passes the start angle on the way down, the app captures a single frame of the built-in display using Apple’s ScreenCaptureKit. It does not record video and captures no audio. LidShift’s own windows and the pointer are left out.
  • Memory only. The frame is turned into a graphics texture for the effect. It is never written to disk, never cached and never leaves your Mac.
  • Released right away. The image is discarded as soon as you reopen the lid, switch effects, pause LidShift, quit it, or when an error occurs.

The captured frame may of course show whatever was on your screen, including personal content. It is processed exclusively on your device, solely to display the effect you selected, and is not accessible to me or anyone else. External displays are never captured.

4. Settings stored on your Mac

So that LidShift remembers your choices, it saves these values in its own sandboxed container on your Mac:

  • the selected effect and whether LidShift is active,
  • the start angle and the Glass settings (refraction, blur, glass, darkening),
  • the ProMotion preference,
  • whether you have completed the first-run guide.

None of these values identify you, and none are transmitted. If you turn on Launch at Login, the app registers itself as a login item with macOS; that entry is managed by macOS and can be removed in System Settings → General → Login Items. Deleting the app together with ~/Library/Containers/com.hildel.LidShift removes all stored settings.

5. No network, no tracking

LidShift contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app’s sandbox entitlements do not allow incoming or outgoing network connections, so it has no way to send data. It does not use input monitoring, accessibility access, a helper tool or any privileged process.

If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in macOS, Apple may provide me with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. That sharing is controlled by you in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Download and distribution

If you obtain LidShift through Apple’s App Store, Apple processes the data needed for the download, your Apple Account and any purchase as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. I receive no personal data about you from that, only aggregated sales and download figures.

7. Contacting me

If you write to me by email — for support, feedback or a compatibility report — I process your email address, your message and anything you include (for example your Mac model or macOS version) to answer it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to using the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). Emails are deleted once the matter is resolved, unless statutory retention periods apply.

8. This website

This website is static and hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, the hosting provider processes technical connection data — IP address, time of request, requested file, referrer and user agent — in server log files, which is technically necessary to deliver the page. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a functioning website).

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. All stylesheets, scripts and images are served from this site itself, and no external fonts are loaded, so no third party receives your IP address when you open a page. Your light/dark preference is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device. The interactive lid demo runs entirely in your browser.

9. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

  • GitHub Inc. — hosting of this website.
  • Apple — App Store distribution and, if you opted in, crash reports and analytics.

These providers may process data in the USA. The transfers are based on the EU standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The app itself passes no data to anyone. Nothing is shared for advertising, and nothing is sold.

10. Retention and your rights

The captured frame lasts only as long as an effect runs; settings stay on your Mac until you delete them. Server logs at the hosting provider are deleted according to its own retention periods.

You have the right to information (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). One email to info@hildel.com is enough. Because LidShift stores no personal data on my side, a request for information will usually be answered with exactly that.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for me that is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

11. Changes to this policy

If LidShift gains features that change how data is handled, this page will be updated before those features ship. The date at the top always shows the current version.
