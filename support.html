Support

LidShift is a one-person project, and that person reads every email.

Get in touch

A bug, a question, a MacBook where LidShift works — or one where it doesn’t — write to info@hildel.com. Answers usually take a day or two.

For problems, these help most: your Mac model and macOS version, the selected effect, and the two status lines under Advanced in the menu bar panel (for example Sensor: Active and Ready — close lid below 90°).

Write an email

Which Macs are supported?

A MacBook running macOS 15.0 or later whose hinge reports a lid angle. Apple doesn’t document that sensor, so there is no official model list. The first-run guide checks it live: if it says Your MacBook is compatible, you’re set. On a Mac without the sensor, LidShift shows Unsupported / unavailable and keeps running harmlessly. Effects only ever appear on the built-in display.

Troubleshooting

The effect doesn’t start when I close the lid.

Check three things in the menu bar panel: the Active switch is on, an effect is selected, and — for every effect except Dim — Screen Recording is allowed. Then open the lid above the start angle (90° by default) so the effect can arm, and close it slowly. The status line under Advanced should read Ready — close lid below 90° before you start.

How do I allow Screen Recording later?

Use Allow screen recording… in the menu bar panel, or run the guide again with Advanced → Getting Started…. You can also switch it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. If macOS asks, quit and reopen LidShift afterwards.

The panel says “Unsupported / unavailable” or “Access denied”.

Unsupported / unavailable means no matching sensor was found — the Mac may not have one, or macOS may be hiding it. Access denied means the sensor exists but couldn’t be read. In both cases, please send your Mac model, macOS version and any error code shown; it helps map which Macs work.

It says “Capture failed” or “Rendering failed”.

LidShift tries one capture per close. Open the lid above the start angle again to retry. If it keeps failing, check the Screen Recording permission and send me the exact message.

My desktop looks frozen while the lid is half closed.

That’s expected: the effect works on a single still image of the moment you crossed the start angle. Your apps keep running underneath, and clicks go straight through the overlay. Open the lid fully and the live desktop is back.

Something is missing from the image.

LidShift leaves out its own windows and the pointer, and macOS may keep protected content — such as some video players — out of any screen capture.

The motion stutters.

Rendering targets 60 FPS while the lid moves. On a ProMotion display, try Advanced → ProMotion · up to 120 FPS. The sensor itself reports about 30 times a second in whole degrees, so very fast movements can still look stepped.

Launch at Login doesn’t stick.

Keep LidShift in a fixed place such as the Applications folder before switching it on. If the panel says the login item needs approval, open Advanced → Login item settings… and allow it there.

Frequently asked

How do I change the effect or when it starts?

Pick the effect in the menu bar panel. Effect Settings (⌘,) sets the start angle between 60° and 120° and, for Glass, refraction, blur, glass and darkening. Restore defaults resets them. Everything is saved automatically.

Does LidShift change my brightness or keep my Mac awake?

No. Dim is a black overlay, not a brightness change, and LidShift never prevents sleep. After waking, the sensor reconnects on its own within a few seconds.

Does anything leave my Mac?

No. The captured frame lives only in memory and is released when you reopen the lid, switch effects, pause or quit. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent, and there is no account, analytics or network access. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I pause or turn it off?

Flip the Active switch in the panel — your chosen effect is kept. Quit LidShift at the bottom of the panel closes the app completely.

How do I remove LidShift?

Turn off Launch at Login, quit the app and move it to the Trash. Its saved settings sit in ~/Library/Containers/com.hildel.LidShift, which you can delete too. The Screen Recording entry can be removed in System Settings.
